OWN YOUR DATA

Data & Settings

Your journal is stored locally in this browser or web-view.

Backup and export

Download a JSON backup for restoring the journal, or CSV for Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.

Import replaces the existing journal only after confirmation. Downloads should be stored in iCloud Drive for safekeeping.

How to use the journal

Record a match shortly after it ends. Over time, compare matchups, lead choices, and decisions. Review at least every ten games instead of overreacting to one loss.

No account, external server, or internet connection is required after opening this file. Data stays on the device/browser where it is entered. Local browser storage can be cleared by iPadOS or browser settings, so export backups regularly.

Reset journal

This permanently erases the battles stored in this browser. Export a backup first.
